# Design pass 1 evidence (#722)

Review aids for the first design pass on
[#722](https://github.com/cbusillo/context-panel/issues/722). They are not
accepted design until the issue records Chris's agreement; accepted decisions
move into `docs/design-direction.md` and SwiftUI code.

- `proposed/`: mockups at real point sizes, captured at 2x (Apple TV at 1x),
  light and dark. Made-up accounts and a fixed time (Thu Oct 1, 2026, 9:12 PM).
- `click-areas/`: the same mockups with each click area outlined.
- `before/`: today's widgets from `Tools/ContextPanelSharedViewRenderer`
  (`dense-accounts` and `reset-visible` fixtures), cropped to the widget frame.
  The other "before" images are the App Store screenshots under
  `Resources/AppStore/Screenshots/`.
- `source/`: the mockup page and its Playwright capture script.
